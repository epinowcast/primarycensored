# Normal delay mean and standard deviation of a pcens object, defaulting as in [`stats::pnorm()`](https://rdrr.io/r/stats/Normal.html)

Normal delay mean and standard deviation of a pcens object, defaulting
as in [`stats::pnorm()`](https://rdrr.io/r/stats/Normal.html)

## Usage

``` r
.norm_mean_sd(object)
```

## Arguments

- object:

  A `pcens` object.

## Value

A list with `mean` and `sd`.
